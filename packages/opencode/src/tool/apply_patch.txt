Applies file additions, updates, moves and deletions described by `patchText`. Relative paths resolve against the active workspace directory.

Patch syntax:
*** Begin Patch
*** Add File: new.txt
+initial content
*** Update File: existing.txt
@@
-old text
+new text
*** Update File: before.txt
*** Move to: after.txt
@@
-old text
+new text
*** Delete File: obsolete.txt
*** End Patch

Each file section starts with its operation header. Added content lines start with `+`; update hunks use `@@` with context, `-` removals and `+` additions. Deletion sections have no content lines.
